# Assumptions and Limitations

This is a **public-source independent assessment**, not an internal audit or consulting engagement.

## No Internal Access

I do not have access to loanDepot:

- internal network diagrams,
- security logs,
- SIEM/EDR telemetry,
- IAM configuration,
- incident-response records,
- vulnerability reports,
- penetration-test results,
- audit workpapers,
- backup logs,
- risk acceptance records,
- insurance policies,
- security policies or procedures.

## No Root-Cause Claim

The public sources reviewed do not identify the technical root cause of the 2024 intrusion. I therefore do not attribute the incident to a specific missing control, product, vulnerability, employee action, vendor, or attack technique.

## Risk Scores Are Analyst Judgment

Likelihood and impact values are analytical estimates created for this assessment.

They are not:

- loanDepot's internal risk scores,
- regulator ratings,
- NIST ratings,
- actuarial predictions,
- probabilities of another breach.

## Publicly Disclosed Controls Are Not Control-Test Results

A statement that a company performs a control does not prove that the control:

- covers every relevant system,
- runs at the expected frequency,
- is configured correctly,
- produces acceptable results,
- is monitored effectively,
- had no exceptions.

That would require internal evidence and testing.

## Time Boundary

The case study uses sources from the January 2024 incident through loanDepot's 2025 Form 10-K filed in March 2026.

Later events could change the analysis.
