# NIST CSF 2.0 Mapping

This project uses NIST CSF 2.0 as a high-level reference.

NIST CSF 2.0 is organized around six Functions:

**Govern → Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover**

The project does not claim formal compliance.

## Why Map Risks to a Framework?

The risk score tells us **what deserves attention**.

The framework mapping helps communicate **what type of cybersecurity outcome should be strengthened, validated, or monitored**.

Example:

- Risk: operational disruption
- Evidence: systems were shut down and later restored
- Risk treatment focus: resilience and recovery
- CSF mapping: PR.IR / RC.RP

That is more useful than adding a framework name to the project without connecting it to decisions.
